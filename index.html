Navigation & Route Instructions

How To Reach Junglee Resort

Follow our exact video guides and navigation advice to reach smoothly without getting stuck.

PLEASE DO NOT FOLLOW GOOGLE MAPS DIRECTLY!

Google Maps calculates the 10 Kms off-road stretch as a river and will divert you to a 45 Kms extra official road detour. Please watch our YouTube route guide video or ask for our step-by-step audio recording before driving!

Route 1: Via Forest Off-Roading (10 Km)

  • Entry point from Ganga Bhogpur (Forest Entry Gate).
  • 10 Km jungle safari-like off-roading path along the natural stream.
  • Only SUV vehicles with 170mm+ ground clearance recommended.
  • Local drivers expert on rough terrain can bring hatchbacks, but guest self-driving requires caution.
  • Parking facility available at Ganga Bhogpur with Resort Pick-Up & Drop option (₹1,500 one way).

Route 2: Via Neelkanth Temple (Easy Route)

  • Drive via Neelkanth Temple Road, Rishikesh.
  • Features only 1.5 Kms easy off-road stretch.
  • Recommended for guests driving standard sedans or small cars.
  • Watch the video tutorial below for full visual reference.

Safety & Forest Protocol

Important Guidelines For Guests

Please read these key rules to ensure a safe, peaceful, and eco-friendly stay in the Tiger Reserve.

1

Forest Barrier Closes @ 7:00 PM

Entry into Rajaji National Park is strictly restricted after 7:00 PM. Guests must cross the barrier near Chandi Devi Temple (Haridwar) or Barrage Barrier (Rishikesh) before 7 PM.

2

Vehicle Ground Clearance

10 km off-roading path inside forest. Vehicles with 170mm+ ground clearance recommended. Pick-up available from Kaudia village if needed.

3

Mobile Connectivity

JIO & Vodafone mobile networks have proper coverage at Junglee Resort for smooth communication.

4

Music & Noise Policy

Loud DJ music is NOT allowed in forest reserve. We provide Bluetooth speakers for low volume background music around bonfire.

5

No Littering In Forest

Guests are requested strictly not to throw plastic or waste from vehicles during drive as it poses severe harm to wildlife fauna.

6

Wildlife Caution

Dense forest with wild Tigers, Leopards, and Elephants. Do not get down from vehicle during jungle drive under any circumstance.

Nearby Attractions

Sightseeing & Wildlife Safari

Discover top eco-tourism destinations around Junglee Resort in Rishikesh & Haridwar.

Open Gypsy Safari

Rajaji National Park safari covers 32 Kms capital Q-shape track starting near Chilla Gate. Home to 37 Tigers, 500+ Wild Elephants, 250+ Leopards, bears, and 450 species of exotic birds! Safari bookings arranged directly from resort.

Lakshman Jhula Rishikesh

Historic suspension bridge over River Ganga built in 1929. Legend says Lord Lakshman crossed Ganga river on jute ropes at this exact spot. Connects Tapovan (Tehri Garhwal) to Jonk (Pauri Garhwal).

Ram Jhula Rishikesh

Iconic suspension bridge constructed in 1986 connecting Swargashram to Shivananda Ashram at Muni Ki Reti. Larger than Lakshman Jhula and surrounded by famous spiritual ashrams.

Parmarth Niketan & Ganga Aarti

Largest Ashram in Rishikesh on Ganga banks (founded 1942). Famous for serene Gurukul atmosphere, daily Yoga sessions, giant Lord Shiva statue, and world-renowned sunset Ganga Aarti.
